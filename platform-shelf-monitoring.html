Platform / Shelf Monitoring

Shelf monitoring: find price mismatches, gaps and failing labels before shoppers do

AiESL turns the status of every connected label, your POS and inventory data and, where installed, cameras and sensors into one view of shelf health — then routes the fixes to store teams with pick-to-light labels.

Get a Custom QuoteWhat it monitors
// THE PROBLEM

What store teams cannot see from the back office

Price mismatches

A label that failed to update, or a price that changed in the POS but not on the shelf, is only found when a shopper complains at the till.

Gaps and out-of-stocks

Empty facings and low stock are discovered by walking the aisles, often after sales are already lost.

An invisible label fleet

Thousands of labels, gateways and batteries — and no single view of which ones are offline, failing or running low.

// WHAT IT MONITORS

One view of shelf health, built from the data you already have

AiESL shelf monitoring combines device status from every connected label, data from your POS and inventory systems, and — where installed — cameras and sensors, to spot price mismatches, stock gaps and planogram drift and turn them into store tasks.

What is detectedWhere the signal comes fromWhat happens next
Labels showing the wrong priceUpdate status reported for every label through the middleware, checked against the POS priceAn alert and a store task
Offline labels, failed updates, low batteriesDevice-layer status monitoring across all connected brands and gatewaysFleet health dashboard; firmware upgrades pushed centrally
Stock gaps and low stockStock levels from ERP / WMS; vision sensors where installedReplenishment task; pick-to-light guides the picker
Planogram driftCameras or vision sensors where installedCorrection task with the location and product
Store trafficAiESL Flow Camera footfall analyticsStaffing and promotion timing

Electronic labels alone cannot see the shelf. For merchandising and planogram compliance, the published evidence is for camera-based systems, not labels — see what the evidence supports.
