Shelf-edge retail media: scheduled, verified promotions on e-paper

Turn electronic shelf labels and colour e-paper displays into promotion surfaces for brand campaigns and shopper offers — scheduled centrally, confirmed label by label and measured against sales.

// THE OPPORTUNITY

Why the shelf edge is worth more than a price

Closest to the decision

The shelf label is the last message a shopper reads before choosing. It is the one in-store surface tied to a single product.

Paper promotions are slow

Printed promo tags and shelf wobblers take days to deploy, are hard to verify and are rarely removed on time.

Brands want proof

Suppliers increasingly expect in-store placements to be scheduled, verifiable and measured like online media.

// FORMATS

Four shelf-edge formats, from a promo badge to a 31.5″ poster

Shelf-edge retail media on AiESL uses e-paper surfaces you may already own — promo colours and QR codes on standard labels, larger colour labels for featured products, and Prism colour e-paper displays for aisle and entrance campaigns.

FormatWhat it showsTypical use
Promo badge on standard labelsSpot colour (usually red), promo price, multi-buy mechanicSupplier-funded price promotions at every facing
QR code on the labelLink to recipes, reviews, manuals or a brand page (can follow GS1 Digital Link)Content and engagement campaigns that are measurable by scans
Large colour labels (4.2″–13.3″)Product image, brand colours, full specificationsFeatured products, endcaps, electronics and premium ranges
Prism colour e-paper displays (12.43″–31.5″)Six-pigment colour images that change remotely and hold with no powerAisle, checkout and entrance campaigns

Prism displays are currently supplied as samples and are not yet certified; retail-system integration is being validated. Details on the Prism promo displays page; label sizes on the products page.

// HOW A CAMPAIGN RUNS

Plan, push, confirm, measure

StepWhat happensData you get
1. PlanChoose stores, aisles and products, the creative template and the promo price.A campaign plan tied to specific labels
2. ScheduleSet start and end times per store or region; promotions switch on at opening and off on time.No promotions left running after the deal ends
3. PushThe platform sends the creative and price to every matching label, across connected brands.—
4. ConfirmEach label reports that it updated.Proof of display per store and facing
5. MeasureCompare sales of promoted products against control stores; add footfall from the Flow Camera where installed.Campaign results you can share with suppliers

The platform provides the mechanics — scheduling, delivery, confirmation and measurement data. How you sell and price shelf media to brands remains your commercial decision.

// WHAT E-PAPER IS NOT

Honest limits: e-paper is a poster, not a screen

E-paper does wellE-paper does not do
Holds an image with no power; readable in any store lightingVideo or animation
Changes remotely in seconds, on scheduleInstant, second-by-second rotation of many ads
Runs on batteries or harvested indoor lightBright, backlit attention-grabbing like LCD
Shows the same price as the till, by designPersonalised offers to individual shoppers

Every promotional price must also match the price charged at checkout — regulators treat promotions as one of the most error-prone areas of shelf pricing. See ESL pricing accuracy.
